SERVICES & CONTACT

Barangay Carmen provides accessible public services, community assistance, and support programs to address the needs of its residents, including documentation, health, safety, and social services. For inquiries, concerns, or requests, residents and visitors may contact the barangay office through the available contact information for immediate assistance.

Barangay Services

Featured Content & Services

Barangay Carmen is committed to providing efficient public services and promoting the welfare, safety, and development of the community.

Barangay Clearance

Apply for Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, Certificate of Indigency, and other official documents.

Health Services

Medical consultations, immunization programs, maternal care, and health awareness activities.

Emergency Response

Disaster preparedness, rescue operations, emergency assistance, and first aid services.

Peace & Order

Community safety through cooperation with the Barangay Peacekeeping Action Team (BPAT).

Community Programs

Programs for youth, senior citizens, women, livelihood, sports, and environmental protection.

Announcements

Stay updated with barangay events, assemblies, advisories, and public notices.
