About Barangay Carmen

Barangay Carmen is one of the largest and most populous urban barangays in Cagayan de Oro. Located on the western side of the city, it serves as an important residential, commercial, and transportation hub. The barangay is known for its busy public market, schools, healthcare facilities, churches, and business establishments, making it a center of daily economic and community activities.

It also provides convenient access to nearby districts through major roads and public transportation routes.
